Glossary

Acceptance test
The check at delivery that the device works and meets the contract, before the buyer accepts it. In Indonesian: uji terima.
Adverse event
An incident in which a device harms, or could harm, a patient or a user. In Indonesian: kejadian tidak diinginkan (KTD).
AKD and AKL
The letters at the start of an Indonesian marketing authorisation number. AKD means the device is made in Indonesia; AKL means it is imported.
Alkes
Short for alat kesehatan, the Indonesian term for medical devices.
Amendment
A formal change to a bidding document, sent to every bidder before the deadline. In Indonesian: adendum.
ASEAN
Association of Southeast Asian Nations. Its Medical Device Directive sets the A to D risk classes that Indonesia follows.
Bidder
A company that submits a bid in a tender. In Indonesian: peserta.
Calibration
Checking a device's measurements against a known reference, and adjusting it if needed.
CDAKB
Cara Distribusi Alat Kesehatan yang Baik: good distribution practice for medical devices, which distributors in Indonesia must follow.
Commissioning
Setting up a newly installed device and testing that it works before it is used. In Indonesian: uji fungsi.
Consumables
Items a device uses up, such as reagents, sensors, filters or paper. In Indonesian: bahan habis pakai.
CPAKB
Cara Pembuatan Alat Kesehatan yang Baik: good manufacturing practice for medical devices, certified for manufacturers in Indonesia.
e-Watch Alkes
Indonesia's national monitoring system for medical devices, run by the Ministry of Health. Problems with devices are reported here.
ECRI
An independent nonprofit organisation in the United States that tests medical devices and reports on their safety.
EMDN
European Medical Device Nomenclature: a system for naming types of device, used mostly in European countries.
GMDN
Global Medical Device Nomenclature: a system for naming types of device, used by regulators in many countries.
HPS
Harga perkiraan sendiri: the cost estimate a government buyer prepares before a tender. It is used to judge the prices offered.
HTA
Health technology assessment: a structured review of what a technology achieves and what it costs. It is used to decide whether a health system should pay for it.
IEC
International Electrotechnical Commission. It publishes safety standards for electrical medical equipment, such as IEC 60601-1.
INAPROC Katalog Elektronik
Indonesia's national electronic catalogue for government purchasing, run by LKPP.
Info Alkes
The Ministry of Health's public database of registered medical devices, manufacturers and distributors.
IPSRS
Instalasi Pemeliharaan Sarana Rumah Sakit: the hospital unit that maintains equipment and buildings.
ISO
International Organization for Standardization. It publishes standards such as ISO 13485 on quality management for device manufacturers.
IVD
In vitro diagnostic device: a device that tests samples taken from the body, such as blood or urine.
Izin edar
Marketing authorisation. A medical device needs one from the Ministry of Health before it can be sold in Indonesia.
JISDOR
Jakarta Interbank Spot Dollar Rate: the daily reference rate for the rupiah against the US dollar, published by Bank Indonesia.
JKN
Jaminan Kesehatan Nasional: Indonesia's national health insurance, run by BPJS Kesehatan.
LKPP
Lembaga Kebijakan Pengadaan Barang/Jasa Pemerintah: Indonesia's government procurement policy agency.
Lot
A part of a tender that is bid for and awarded separately, often one type of device or one region. In Indonesian: paket.
Model specification
A specification for a type of device that names no brand. It is a starting point that buyers adapt to their own needs.
Particular standard
A standard in the IEC 60601-2 series with extra requirements for one type of medical electrical equipment.
Pokja Pemilihan
The selection working group that runs a government tender. It prepares the bidding document, answers bidders' questions and evaluates the bids.
PONED
Pelayanan Obstetri Neonatal Emergensi Dasar: basic emergency care for mothers and newborns, offered by designated puskesmas.
PPK
Pejabat Pembuat Komitmen: the official who sets the technical specification and the cost estimate (HPS), and signs and manages the contract.
Pre-bid meeting
A meeting where the buyer explains the tender and answers questions from possible bidders. In Indonesian: pemberian penjelasan.
Puskesmas
Pusat kesehatan masyarakat: the community health centre that provides primary care in Indonesia.
Reagent
A chemical that a laboratory analyzer uses to carry out a test. Reagents are bought again and again.
Reference price
A price range for a device, built from cited sources, each with what it includes. It is used to judge the prices offered.
Risk class
One of four classes, A to D, that group medical devices from low to high risk.
Scoring on quality and price
An evaluation method that gives points for the quality of a bid as well as its price. In Indonesian: sistem nilai.
Supplier
A company that sells and delivers goods. In Indonesian government procurement: penyedia.
Test report
A document from a testing laboratory showing that a device was tested against a standard, and the results.
TKDN
Tingkat Komponen Dalam Negeri: the certified share of local content in a product, as a percentage.
Total cost of ownership
Everything a device costs over its working life: purchase, delivery, installation, consumables, maintenance, training and disposal.
UMDNS
Universal Medical Device Nomenclature System: a system for naming types of device, maintained by ECRI.
UNFPA
United Nations Population Fund, the United Nations agency for sexual and reproductive health.
UNICEF
United Nations Children's Fund. It buys medical equipment for many countries and publishes specifications and prices.
UPS
Uninterruptible power supply: a battery unit that keeps a device running during a power cut.
WHO
World Health Organization, the United Nations agency for health.
