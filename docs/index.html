Ask for the right device. Pay a fair price. Run a tender that works.

SistemSehat is free, independent guidance on buying medical equipment for Indonesia's public health facilities. It covers what to ask for, what it should cost, and how to run the tender. The name means “healthy system” in Indonesian.

How to use this site

  1. Find your task

    Choose what you need to do from the list below, or open the guide that matches it.

  2. Read the rules

    Each rule says what to do, gives an example, and ends with a question to ask yourself.

  3. Use the checklist

    Take all 35 questions to your planning meeting or document review, on screen or on paper.

What do you need to do?

  • Write a specification
  • Set a budget or cost estimate I can explain
  • Check the documents in a bid
  • Run a tender again after it failed
  • Judge a new technology a supplier is offering
  • Get a checklist for a meeting

Three questions every purchase must answer

All guides

Answer them in this order. A weak answer to one question cannot be fixed by the next one.

  • 1

    What should we ask for?

    A specification decides what arrives at the facility. Seven rules for writing one that several manufacturers can meet and clinicians can use.

    Specification guide

  • 2

    What should it cost?

    A price means little until you know what it includes. Six rules for building a reference price you can explain and defend.

    Pricing guide

  • 3

    How do we buy it without a failed tender?

    How you divide the lots, score the bids and set the deadlines decides the result before any bid is opened. Seven rules for running the tender.

    Process guide

Two guides that support all three

Like the annexes of a bidding document, they give the background that all three answers depend on.

  • A

    What must a device comply with?

    Marketing authorisation, risk class, quality certificates, standards, local content and calibration. Eight rules for checking the documents.

    Regulation guide

  • B

    How do we judge the technology?

    How it works, what evidence supports it, its history in use and whether it suits your facility. Seven rules for judging a device you did not design.

    Technology guide

Device dossiers

About dossiers

A dossier is a short report on one device. It answers all three questions for that device: a model specification, a reference price range, and lessons for running the purchase. The first three are being written.

How this site stays independent

How it works

  • Public sources only

    Every figure and requirement comes from a document anyone can download, and each one is cited.

  • No money from sellers

    No funding, gifts or sponsored content from manufacturers or distributors.

  • No current tenders

    Nothing is published about a tender that is still being evaluated.

  • Public corrections

    When something here is wrong or outdated, it is corrected and the change is noted on the page.
