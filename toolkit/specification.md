## How to use it

1. Start from the clinical need ([rule 1.1](../specification.html#rule-1-1)), and keep every section.
2. Write what the device must achieve, never a brand or model ([rule 1.2](../specification.html#rule-1-2)).
3. For each requirement, name the document that proves it and the check at delivery ([rule 1.7](../specification.html#rule-1-7)).
