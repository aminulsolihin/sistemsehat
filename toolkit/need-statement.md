## How to use it

1. Write one need statement for each problem you observed ([rule C.2](../identify.html#rule-c-2)). Name no solution.
2. Compare your needs side by side, and choose one ([rule C.3](../identify.html#rule-c-3)).
3. List the criteria any solution must meet, and where each one comes from ([rule C.4](../identify.html#rule-c-4)).
